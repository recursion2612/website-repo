About

My work sits at the intersection of experimental quantum physics and computational tooling. My research background spans open quantum systems and continuous-time quantum walks, and I now focus on the design and simulation of superconducting quantum chips — from device architecture through electromagnetic simulation to the software pipelines that support it.

Experience & research

Knowledge Associate — C-DAC, Pune

Current

Designing a 5-qubit superconducting quantum chip with a ring-topology transmon architecture, using SQUID-based asymmetric tunable transmons, dispersive readout via λ/4 CPW resonators, and frequency-division multiplexing. Building simulation pipelines with Qiskit Metal, Ansys HFSS, Q3D, and PyAEDT for eigenmode and driven-modal analysis.
