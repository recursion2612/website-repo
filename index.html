About

My work sits at the intersection of experimental quantum physics and computational tooling. My research background spans open quantum systems and continuous-time quantum walks, and I now focus on the design and simulation of superconducting quantum chips — from device architecture through electromagnetic simulation to the software pipelines that support it.
